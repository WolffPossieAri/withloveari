### Ingredients

#### Banana Bundt Cake

* 1 cup (2 sticks) salted butter, softened
* 1 cup granulated sugar
* 1 cup light brown sugar, packed
* 2 teaspoons vanilla extract
* 2 large eggs
* 1½ cups buttermilk, room temperature
* 4 ripe bananas, mashed
* 3 cups all-purpose flour
* 2 teaspoons baking soda
* ½ teaspoon kosher salt

#### Cream Cheese Frosting

* 8 ounces cream cheese, softened
* 1½ cups powdered sugar
* 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
* ½ teaspoon kosher salt
* 2 tablespoons heavy cream (or milk)
* 1 teaspoon ground cinnamon

**Optional Garnish**

* A light sprinkle of ground cinnamon

### Instructions

Preheat your oven to **350°F**. Generously grease a Bundt pan, then lightly dust it with flour to help ensure the cake releases easily after baking.

<img src="/images/recipes/banana_bunt/floured_pan.jpeg">

In the bowl of a stand mixer fitted with the paddle attachment, cream together the softened butter, granulated sugar, and brown sugar on medium speed for **3–5 minutes**, or until the mixture is light, fluffy, and pale in color. Scrape down the sides of the bowl as needed.

<img src="/images/recipes/banana_bunt/butter_sugar.jpeg">

Add the vanilla extract and eggs, one at a time, mixing on low speed until fully incorporated.

<img src="/images/recipes/banana_bunt/add_eggs.jpeg">

Next, pour in the room-temperature buttermilk and mix until smooth. Add the flour, baking soda, and salt, mixing on low speed just until the dry ingredients are incorporated. Be careful not to overmix, as this helps keep the cake soft and tender.

<img src="/images/recipes/banana_bunt/mixture.jpeg">

In a separate bowl, mash the ripe bananas with a fork until mostly smooth. Add the mashed bananas to the batter and mix on low speed for about **30 seconds**, or just until everything is evenly combined.

Pour the batter into the prepared Bundt pan, spreading it evenly. Bake for **45–55 minutes**, or until a toothpick inserted into the center comes out clean or with just a few moist crumbs attached.

<img src="/images/recipes/banana_bunt/filled_pan.jpeg">

Allow the cake to cool in the pan for **15 minutes**, then carefully invert it onto a serving plate or cake stand. Let it cool completely before adding the frosting.

<img src="/images/recipes/banana_bunt/finished.jpeg">

### Cream Cheese Frosting

While the cake cools, prepare the frosting. Using a stand mixer or handheld mixer, beat the softened cream cheese until smooth and creamy, about **1 minute**.

Add the powdered sugar, vanilla extract, cinnamon, and heavy cream. Beat until the frosting is smooth and fluffy. If you'd like a thinner consistency, simply add another splash of heavy cream until it reaches your desired texture.

Once the cake has completely cooled, spread the cream cheese frosting over the top, allowing it to gently drip down the sides if desired. Finish with a light sprinkle of cinnamon for an extra cozy touch.

<img src="/images/recipes/banana_bunt/finished2.jpeg">

### Storage

Store the cake covered at room temperature for up to **2 days**, or in the refrigerator for up to **5 days**. For the best flavor and texture, let refrigerated slices come to room temperature before serving.
