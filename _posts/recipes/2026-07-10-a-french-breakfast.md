## Ingredients

### Cinnamon French Toast

* 2 tablespoons unsalted butter (for cooking)
* 1 loaf cinnamon-sugar swirl bread, preferably unsliced
* 6 large eggs
* ½ cup whole milk (1% or 2% milk works as well)
* 1 teaspoon ground cinnamon
* ¾ teaspoon salt
* 1½ teaspoons vanilla extract
* 1 tablespoon packed light brown sugar

### Homemade Caramel Syrup

* ½ cup (1 stick) unsalted butter
* 1 cup packed light brown sugar
* ½ cup heavy cream
* 2 teaspoons vanilla extract

### Homemade Vanilla Whipped Cream

* ½ cup heavy whipping cream
* 2 tablespoons powdered sugar
* ½ teaspoon vanilla extract

### For Serving

* Fresh strawberries, sliced
* Fresh blueberries, blackberries, or your favorite berry medley
* Extra maple syrup or caramel syrup (optional)

## Instructions

### Homemade Caramel Syrup

In a small saucepan over medium heat, combine the butter, brown sugar, and heavy cream. Cook for **5–8 minutes**, stirring occasionally, until the mixture is smooth and slightly thickened. Remove from the heat, stir in the vanilla extract, and set aside to cool slightly while you prepare the French toast.

### Cinnamon French Toast

Using a serrated knife, slice the cinnamon swirl bread into **8 thick slices**, about **¾ to 1 inch** thick.

In a large mixing bowl, whisk together the eggs, milk, cinnamon, salt, vanilla extract, and brown sugar until the mixture is smooth and well combined.

<img src="/images/recipes/the_french_way/milk.jpeg">
<img src="/images/recipes/the_french_way/mixture.jpeg">

Preheat an electric griddle to **325°F**, or heat a large skillet over medium heat. Dip each slice of bread into the custard mixture, coating both sides evenly. Allow any excess batter to drip off before placing it on the hot cooking surface.

Melt **1 tablespoon of butter** on the griddle or skillet. Arrange the coated slices on the hot surface, leaving a little space between each piece so they cook evenly. Cook for **3–4 minutes per side**, or until each slice is beautifully golden brown and lightly crisp around the edges.

<img src="/images/recipes/the_french_way/cooking.jpeg">

Transfer the cooked French toast to a wire rack or serving platter. If cooking in batches, carefully wipe the skillet clean with a paper towel and melt the remaining tablespoon of butter before adding more slices. French toast is best enjoyed fresh from the griddle, as letting it sit too long can cause it to lose its crisp exterior.

<img src="/images/recipes/the_french_way/fished.jpeg">

### Homemade Vanilla Whipped Cream

While the French toast cooks, prepare the whipped cream. In a chilled mixing bowl, combine the heavy whipping cream and powdered sugar. Using a stand mixer or hand mixer, beat on high speed until medium to stiff peaks form, about **1–3 minutes**. Be careful not to overmix. Gently fold in the vanilla extract and, if desired, a tiny pinch of salt to enhance the flavor.

### To Serve

Stack the warm French toast onto serving plates and top with a generous dollop of homemade vanilla whipped cream. Add fresh sliced strawberries, blueberries, blackberries, or your favorite berry medley, then finish with a warm drizzle of homemade caramel syrup.

Serve immediately and enjoy every cozy, cinnamon filled bite.
